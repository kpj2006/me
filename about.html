About me

The longer story, for anyone who wants it.

I'm a B.Tech CSE student at LNMIIT, Jaipur (started 2024). I started open source with AOSSIE and have maintained AOSSIE projects since February 2025.

In 2026 I was an LFX mentee at Magma and a Rootstock Rootcamp builder. Then, in GSoC'26, I became AOSSIE's DevOps Lead and brought shared CI/CD templates, reusable GitHub Actions and security scans to the whole org.

How I work: first learn what the community already solved, then design how the pieces fit, then automate it with CI/CD and agents.
